How this record works

Every pick is logged at the odds when it's released, and losses are never removed.
  • Picks are recorded before the game starts, with odds, units and tier (VIP or Free).
  • Results are graded the next morning. Pending picks stay marked as pending until they're graded.
  • Units: a win pays units × odds/100 on plus odds or units × 100/|odds| on minus odds, a loss costs the units risked, and a push is 0.
  • ROI = net units ÷ units risked on graded wins and losses.
