How it works

From post to permanent record

Three steps, the same every day. Nothing gets picked out after the fact.

  1. 01

    Plays drop in the free Discord

    Alago posts each play in the AlagoBets Discord with the sport, pick, odds and units.

  2. 02

    Logged automatically at release odds

    The play is written to this record the moment it's posted, before the event starts. No edits afterwards.

  3. 03

    Graded every morning

    Results are graded the next morning and the record, units and ROI update. Losses stay, permanently.

Methodology

The rules behind the record

These rules don't change when results are bad. Every pick is logged at the odds when it's released, and losses are never removed.

Same math for every pick, every sport

Odds
Logged at release. Lines are never adjusted after the fact.
Timing
Every play is logged before the event starts.
Losses
Never removed. Wins, losses, pushes and voids stay in the log permanently.
Grading
Every morning, for the previous day's events.
Units
Win (plus odds): units × odds / 100 Win (minus odds): units × 100 / |odds|Loss: −units · Push: 0
ROI
net units ÷ units risked on graded wins and losses
Win rate
wins ÷ (wins + losses), pushes excluded
