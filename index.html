Methodology

How picks are logged and graded

1

Logged before kickoff

Each pick is recorded when it's released, before the event starts, with the odds and units.

2

Release odds, no edits

Every pick is logged at the odds when it's released. Lines are never adjusted after the fact.

3

Losses are never removed

Wins, losses, pushes and voids all stay in the log permanently. Nothing gets deleted.

4

Graded every morning

Results are graded the next morning. A win pays units × odds/100 (plus odds) or units × 100/|odds| (minus odds); a loss costs the units risked; a push is 0. ROI = net units ÷ units risked.
